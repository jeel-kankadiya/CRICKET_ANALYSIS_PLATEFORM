Model Transparency

How Predictive Is Pre-Match Data, Really?

Honest finding, not a bug: the best model reaches a cross-validated ROC-AUC around 0.49–0.57 — barely above a coin flip. This matches well-documented research on T20 cricket: a 20-over match is short enough that toss luck, individual big-hitting overs, and in-game variance dominate over pre-match team strength. Pre-match features (Elo, form, venue, toss) genuinely cap out here without ball-by-ball live data. The value of this platform is therefore less "certain prediction" and more calibrated probability + transparent analytics — which is the professionally honest framing for this data.
