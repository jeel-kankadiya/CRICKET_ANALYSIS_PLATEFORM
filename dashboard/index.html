Why ~54% Pre-Match Accuracy Is Expected — Not A Bug

T20 cricket is an inherently high-variance, short format — a single over can completely reverse a match. Pre-match team-strength signals (Elo, form, head-to-head) have genuine predictive power but are fundamentally capped by the sport's randomness. Our models were validated chronologically using TimeSeriesSplit (no future data leaks into training). The value is calibrated, transparent probability estimation — not an overconfident "certain winner" predictor. The live in-match model (77.6% accuracy) uses real-time ball-by-ball data and substantially outperforms pre-match predictions, as expected.
